Quick answer

How does a drone work?

A drone flies on six core parts working together: a frame that holds everything, four brushless motors with propellers that create thrust, ESCs that power the motors, a flight controller that decides how fast each motor spins, a GPS that gives position, and a battery. Sensors such as IMUs and barometers tell the flight controller how the drone is moving, and it corrects the motors many times a second to stay stable.

FAQ

Drone electronics: your questions answered

Short, direct answers about drone parts, sensors and how to choose the right stack.

What are the main parts of a drone?

The main parts of a drone are the frame, brushless motors with propellers, electronic speed controllers (ESCs), a flight controller, a GPS module and a battery. Many drones also carry a camera. Together they provide structure, thrust, control, navigation and power.

What does a flight controller do in a drone?

A flight controller is the drone’s brain. It reads sensors such as IMUs and barometers, compares them with the pilot’s or mission’s commands, and sets how fast each motor should spin, many times a second, to keep the drone stable and on course.

What is an ESC in a drone?

An ESC (electronic speed controller) converts battery power into the three-phase current a brushless motor needs. It takes the throttle signal from the flight controller and controls motor speed. Each motor has one ESC, or four share a single 4-in-1 board.

What is AM32 firmware?

AM32 is open-source firmware for drone ESCs. Because it is open, builders can configure and update the ESC themselves instead of being locked into one vendor’s software. All Skydrive ESCs from Space and Tech Engineering run AM32.

What sensors does a drone use?

A drone typically uses an IMU (gyroscope and accelerometer) to sense rotation and acceleration, a barometer for altitude, a GNSS/GPS receiver for position and speed, and current and voltage sensors to monitor the motors and battery. Cameras are optional payloads.

Why does a drone need GPS?

GPS tells the drone where it is and how fast it is moving. It enables position hold, return-to-home and autonomous waypoint missions. Without GPS a drone can still fly manually but cannot hold position by itself.

What is a triple-redundant flight controller?

A triple-redundant flight controller carries three independent IMUs so that one failing or noisy sensor does not have to end the flight. The Aazmuth flight controller has 3 IMUs and 2 barometers, each on its own isolated bus.

Which flight controller, ESC and GPS suit an agriculture drone?

For agriculture, Space and Tech Engineering suggests the Aazmuth flight controller, Skydrive AM32 60A ESCs for heavy sprayer payloads and the NAVSATE GPS for UAVs. Isolated sensors help tolerate vibration and field conditions.

Which electronics suit an FPV racing drone?

For compact FPV builds, use the Exelr8 F7 flight controller with the Skydrive 55A AM32 4-in-1 ESC and the NAVSATE mini GPS. A single 4-in-1 ESC keeps wiring short on 5-inch builds and below.
