Product Regulatory Disclaimer

Our products are parts of drones, not complete drones. This page explains what that means for regulation.

Effective: 16 November 2026. Last updated: 16 November 2026.

1. Components are not aircraft

Space and Tech Engineering LLP supplies electronic components and accessories for unmanned aircraft systems. The supply of an individual component does not constitute certification or approval of a complete drone or unmanned aircraft system. Customers and system integrators are responsible for verifying applicable regulatory, safety and operational requirements before deployment.

2. What we supply

  • Flight controllers, ESCs (speed controllers) and GPS modules: electronic components for integrators and builders.
  • Radio transmitters (planned, not yet released): radio equipment with its own approval requirements.
  • We do not sell complete unmanned aircraft systems on this site.

3. Drone rules in India

Complete drones and their operators are subject to the Drone Rules, 2021 and later amendments, the Certification Scheme for Unmanned Aircraft Systems, 2022 and DGCA notifications. Type certification, registration, remote pilot requirements and airspace permissions apply to the final aircraft and its operator. The final drone manufacturer and operator are responsible for them.

4. Radio equipment

Radio transmitters and links may need WPC Equipment Type Approval or other authorisation, and must use permitted frequencies and power. We do not state that any radio product is approved or legal to operate unless the approval is documented and listed on this page.

5. Our approvals status

As of the effective date we do not claim DGCA, WPC or other regulatory approval for any product on this website. If we obtain one, we will name the product and approval here only with supporting documents.

6. Questions

Ask the Grievance Officer or write to us through the contact page and we will tell you what we can document for a specific product.
