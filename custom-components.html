Custom electronics for your drone build

Order custom drone components from Space and Tech Engineering in India. We review requests for new components and changes to existing boards, from prototype quantities to OEM batches.

Component requests we review

  • Flight controllers with a different board size, mounting pattern or connector layout.
  • ESCs for a specific supply voltage, motor current or installation space.
  • GPS modules with a particular connector or interface for your flight controller.

Send your requirements

Tell us what the part needs to do, which drone it goes into, how many units you need and when you need them. Include any fixed requirements, such as dimensions, pinout or firmware compatibility. If you already have a drawing or reference part number, include it with your enquiry.

What happens after you enquire?

We review your requirements and follow up if we need more details. If we can take on the order, we send a written quote with the agreed specifications, price, lead time, warranty and any minimum order quantity before you confirm.
