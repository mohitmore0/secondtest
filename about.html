Built in INDIA. Flown everywhere.

We are a small team of hardware and firmware engineers making flight controllers, ESCs and GPS modules that Indian builders can actually get support for.

Mission

Make dependable flight electronics in India, with open docs and support that builders can actually reach.

Vision

An Indian drone industry that flies on locally designed, locally supported hardware.

How we work

  • Test everything. Every batch gets bench time and flight time before it ships.
  • Write it down. Public docs, pinouts and changelogs, so you are never guessing.
  • Make it here. Designed in India, so a fix is a phone call away.

Our story

  1. 2022: The first board. Two engineers, one soldering station and a flight controller that finally held a hover.
  2. 2023: Skydrive ESCs. AM32 speed controllers go into small batches and onto builders' benches.
  3. 2024: NAVSATE GPS. A GPS module tuned for UAVs, tested on long, boring, wonderful flights.
  4. 2025: Aazmuth. Our modular, triple-redundant flagship controller leaves the lab.
