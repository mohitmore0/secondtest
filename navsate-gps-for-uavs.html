NAVSATE GPS for UAVs FAQ

What does the NAVSATE GPS do?

It tells the flight controller where the drone is and how fast it is moving, which enables position hold, return-to-home and waypoint missions.

Which flight controller works with NAVSATE?

It is designed as part of our stack with the Aazmuth flight controller. Share your build and we will confirm wiring and settings.
