NAVSATE GPS Module for UAV Drones

GPS

A DroneCAN GPS module with an onboard magnetometer for UAV positioning. Compare it for flight controllers that support CAN peripherals.

NAVSATE is our GPS module tuned for UAVs and tested on long flights. It gives your flight controller the position and ground-speed data it needs for position hold, return-to-home and waypoint missions, and pairs with Aazmuth for survey, agriculture and defence work.

What to check before ordering

Confirm the controller supports DroneCAN and check the connector, available CAN port and mounting space. Position hold, return-to-home and waypoint behavior depend on your flight firmware and configuration.

NAVSATE GPS for UAVs FAQ

What does the NAVSATE GPS do?

It tells the flight controller where the drone is and how fast it is moving, which enables position hold, return-to-home and waypoint missions.

Which flight controller works with NAVSATE?

It is designed as part of our stack with the Aazmuth flight controller. Share your build and we will confirm wiring and settings.
