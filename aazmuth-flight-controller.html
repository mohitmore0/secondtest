Aazmuth STM32H7 UAV Flight Controller

Flight controllers

Designed in India for professional UAVs, Aazmuth has three IMUs and two barometers on separate buses. Its modular design lets you replace an IMU board without replacing the controller.

Aazmuth is our flagship flight controller for professional UAVs. It runs on an STM32 H7 (Cortex-M7, up to 480 MHz) and carries 3 IMUs and 2 barometers, each on its own isolated bus, so one noisy or failed sensor does not take down the flight. IMU boards plug in over 20-pin Aazmuth Bus connectors, which means you can swap or upgrade sensors without replacing the controller. Built for defence, industry, agriculture, survey, startups and universities.

Highlights

  • Triple IMU and dual barometer redundancy
  • Isolated sensor buses, so vibration on one does not corrupt the others
  • Modular IMU boards on the 20-pin Aazmuth Bus
  • Public pinout, calibration and sensor-integration docs

What to check before ordering

For a survey or agriculture UAV, compare the 92 x 54 x 14.6 mm enclosure and 63 g weight with your airframe. Confirm the firmware, peripheral connections and supported sensor modules for your build.

Aazmuth flight controller FAQ

What redundancy does the Aazmuth flight controller have?

Aazmuth carries 3 IMUs and 2 barometers, each on its own isolated bus, so a fault or vibration on one sensor does not corrupt the others.

What processor does Aazmuth use?

An STM32 H7 (Cortex-M7) running at up to 480 MHz, with 2 MB of flash and 1 MB of RAM.

How big and heavy is it?

92 x 54 x 14.6 mm and 63 g.
