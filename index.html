Indian drone electronics, designed in India

Space and Tech Engineering LLP is a small team of hardware and firmware engineers building flight electronics for drones. Our first flight controller, built by two engineers in 2022, finally held a hover. Since then we have added Skydrive AM32 ESCs, NAVSATE GPS modules and the Aazmuth flagship flight controller.

Our mission is to make dependable flight electronics in India, with open documentation and support that builders can actually reach. We design our boards and firmware at our offices in India, so a question or a fix is a phone call away. We work with drone manufacturers, UAV companies, FPV builders, research teams and OEM buyers.

Flight controllers, ESCs and GPS for UAV and FPV drones

Four product families that are designed to work together, each with its own documentation.

  • Aazmuth UAV flight controller: Our flagship flight controller for professional UAVs runs an STM32 H7 (Cortex-M7, up to 480 MHz) with 3 IMUs and 2 barometers, each on an isolated bus. IMU boards plug into the 20-pin Aazmuth Bus, so sensors can be swapped or upgraded without replacing the controller.
  • Exelr8 F7 FPV flight controller: An F7 flight controller for FPV racing and freestyle builds. See its specifications and matching GPS options before choosing components for your stack.
  • AM32 drone ESCs: Compare 3–6S and 6–12S electronic speed controllers, including single-channel ESCs for UAVs and 4-in-1 boards for FPV builds.
  • NAVSATE GPS modules: A UAV GPS module tuned for UAVs and tested on long flights. It gives your flight controller the position data it needs for position hold, return-to-home and waypoint missions. The compact NAVSATE mini is made for FPV drones.

Industries and applications

Where builders use our drone electronics today.

  • Agriculture: Spraying and crop-mapping drones need a steady hover and long, GPS-locked runs.
  • Survey and mapping: Redundant sensors and a reliable GPS keep survey lines straight.
  • Defence and security: Triple-redundant sensing for missions where a failure is not an option.
  • FPV racing and freestyle: Light, fast F7 control and 4-in-1 ESCs for tight, punchy builds.
  • Education and research: Modular boards and open docs that students can open up and learn from.
  • Startups and prototypes: Small batches, direct support and parts that match your build.

How we build

  • Design: Schematics, PCBs and firmware drawn in India.
  • Bench test: Every board is powered, loaded and measured before it leaves the bench.
  • Fly test: Real flights, real logs, real fixes.
  • Ship and support: Public docs, and a phone number that gets answered.

Why choose Space and Tech Engineering

  • Designed and supported in India: Our hardware and firmware engineers sit in India. When something needs fixing, you speak to the people who built it.
  • Tested before it ships: Every batch gets bench time and flight time before it ships.
  • Documentation you can read: We publish pinouts, calibration guides and changelogs, so you are never guessing.
  • Redundancy where it matters: Aazmuth carries three IMUs and two barometers on isolated buses, so one noisy sensor does not take down the flight.
