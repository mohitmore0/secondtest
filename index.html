Indian flight controllers, ESCs and GPS for drones

Triple-redundant Aazmuth flight controllers, Skydrive AM32 ESCs and NAVSATE GPS modules. Designed in Mumbai and Nashik, tested in flight, supported by the engineers who built them.

Space and Tech Engineering LLP is an Indian drone electronics company with offices in Mumbai and Nashik. It designs and sells the Aazmuth and Exelr8 flight controllers, Skydrive AM32 ESCs and NAVSATE GPS modules for UAV and FPV builders.

  • Skydrive AM32 60A 3-6S: 60A AM32 ESC for 3-6S LiPo builds. Handles heavy payloads such as sprayer drones.

Questions answered

What is AM32 firmware?

AM32 is open-source firmware for 32-bit brushless ESCs. Skydrive ESCs run it, so you can configure and update them with the usual AM32 tools.

Which ESC should I buy for an agriculture or sprayer drone?

Start with the Skydrive AM32 60A 3-6S, which is meant for heavier payloads. Share your motor, prop and battery setup and we will confirm the fit.

Which flight controller should I pick?

Aazmuth for redundancy and professional work, Exelr8 F7 for FPV builds. Tell us your build and we will confirm.

Are your drone electronics made in India?

They are designed in Mumbai and Nashik by our own hardware and firmware engineers, so support and fixes are a phone call away.
