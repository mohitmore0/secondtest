4-in-1 ESC 80A 3-6S

ESCs

The 4-in-1 ESC puts four motor-drive channels on a single board for FPV quadcopters and multirotors. It runs AM32 firmware, supports DShot with bidirectional control, and includes crash protection plus reverse-voltage and short-circuit protection. Catalogue rating is 80 A continuous.

Highlights

  • DShot protocol support

  • Crash-protection feature
  • Reverse-voltage and short-circuit protection
  • AM32 firmware supported
