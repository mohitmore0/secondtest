Skydrive AM32 40A 3-6S FAQ

Which firmware does the Skydrive 40A run?

AM32, configurable with standard AM32 tools.

40A or 60A Skydrive ESC?

Pick 40 A for lighter survey and general UAV builds, and 60 A for heavy payloads such as sprayer drones.
