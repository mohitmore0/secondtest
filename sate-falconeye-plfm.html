SATE FalconEye PLFM Beam-Steering Radar

Radar - coming soon

A modular beam-steering radar platform under development, with the AERIS-10N and AERIS-10E variants listed on the product page.

SATE FalconEye PLFM is a modular electronic beam-steering radar platform, currently under development. It comes in two versions: AERIS-10N (Nexus) with a 3 km range and an 8 x 16 patch antenna array, and AERIS-10E (Extended) with a 20 km range and a 32 x 16 dielectric-filled slotted waveguide array. Beam steering covers plus or minus 45 degrees in elevation and azimuth. An onboard FPGA handles pulse compression, Doppler FFT, MTI and CFAR, and a Python GUI with map integration and GPS/IMU position and attitude correction ties it together.

Highlights

  • Two versions: AERIS-10N (Nexus) and AERIS-10E (Extended)
