Shipping, Returns, Refunds and Warranty Policy

This policy covers orders placed directly with Space and Tech Engineering LLP. Agreed order terms apply subject to mandatory law.

Effective: 26 November 2026. Last updated: 26 November 2026.

1. Delivery checks

Check the parcel, SKU, quantity and visible condition on receipt. Where practical, report transit damage, shortage or mismatch within 48 hours so courier evidence can be preserved. Keep the label and packaging if available. Report defects found later promptly within the applicable warranty or legal period. The 48-hour request does not cut off those remedies.

2. Cancellation and change of mind

Request cancellation in writing before dispatch or before custom work or procurement begins. A confirmed order may be cancelled with our written approval or where law permits. Change-of-mind, buyer selection errors and custom or special-order returns require written approval unless law or the accepted order provides otherwise. Any cancellation or restocking charge must have been disclosed and agreed before confirmation and be lawful. Where required for consumer e-commerce, we bear similar charges if we cancel unilaterally. If we cannot fulfil an order, we refund the unfulfilled portion unless you agree to an alternative.

3. Commercial warranty

Your accepted quotation specifies any commercial warranty, its duration, start date and covered items. Commercial warranty covers verified manufacturing defects within the agreed scope. Damage caused by incorrect wiring or polarity, use outside published limits, electrostatic discharge, impact, liquid, misuse, improper installation or unauthorised modification or repair is excluded. We assess whether the condition caused the reported fault. Any paid diagnostic or repair work needs your approval of the charge in advance.

4. Request a return

Contact our Grievance Officer at arup@spaceandtech-engineerings.com with your order or invoice reference, affected SKU and quantity, serial or batch number if available, and a description of the issue. Include relevant photos, packaging or test details where available. We may request reasonable additional evidence and send return authorisation or pickup instructions. Alternative evidence is accepted for assessment. An unboxing video and original packaging are not mandatory. Do not power up visibly damaged goods for evidence.

5. Inspection and remedies

We verify the item against order and serial records and may test it to establish the fault and cause. We provide our decision and reasons in writing. We may decline duplicate, fraudulent or unsubstantiated claims after review. For eligible commercial warranty claims, we may repair or replace the affected unit first and refund the eligible amount if neither is reasonably available. Statutory remedies for defective, deficient, spurious or misdescribed goods and qualifying late deliveries remain available. Any force majeure exception applies only where permitted by law.

6. Freight and refund payment

For confirmed defects, incorrect supply or other claims where we are responsible, we arrange pickup or reimburse reasonable return freight, subject to applicable law. Freight and any lawful deduction for a discretionary return are agreed before return. We initiate approved refunds within seven working days of approval or sooner if required by law. Refunds go to the original payment method where possible or to a verified account agreed with the payer. Store credit is optional. Bank settlement times may vary.

7. Review and statutory rights

Request review through Grievance Redressal using your claim reference and any additional evidence. Mandatory rights remain available regardless of any commercial warranty or standard return condition.
