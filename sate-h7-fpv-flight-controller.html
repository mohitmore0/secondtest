SATE-H7 FPV flight controller

Flight controllers - coming soon

SATE-H7 is an H7-based flight controller for FPV drones. It pairs an STM32H7 microcontroller with a fourth-generation InvenSense gyroscope and accelerometer, an Infineon barometer and a high-performance OSD. Separate 10 V and 5 V BEC outputs and a solder-less plug-and-play design keep builds clean, and it works with digital HD systems.

Highlights

  • Powerful H7-based STM32 microcontroller
  • Separate BEC outputs
  • Solder-less plug-and-play design
  • Multi-firmware support
  • Reverse-voltage and short-circuit protection
  • Compatible with digital HD systems

SATE-H7 FPV flight controller FAQ

SATE-H7 or Exelr8?

SATE-H7 uses an STM32H7 for more processing headroom. Exelr8 uses an STM32F7.

What battery can it run on?

3-6S.
