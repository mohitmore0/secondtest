### Aazmuth flight controller

- Category: Flight controllers
- Page: https://www.spaceandtechengineerings.com/aazmuth-flight-controller.html
- Image: https://static.wixstatic.com/media/e688f6_4ef9d2c2ab6a43a5ab35ac564c0604cc~mv2.jpg

Modular, triple-redundant STM32 H7 flight controller with 3 IMUs and 2 barometers on isolated buses.

Aazmuth is our flagship flight controller for professional UAVs. It runs on an STM32 H7 (Cortex-M7, up to 480 MHz) and carries 3 IMUs and 2 barometers, each on its own isolated bus, so one noisy or failed sensor does not take down the flight. IMU boards plug in over 20-pin Aazmuth Bus connectors, which means you can swap or upgrade sensors without replacing the controller. Built for defence, industry, agriculture, survey, startups and universities.

**Highlights**

- Triple IMU and dual barometer redundancy
- Isolated sensor buses, so vibration on one does not corrupt the others
- Modular IMU boards on the 20-pin Aazmuth Bus
- Public pinout, calibration and sensor-integration docs

**Specifications**

- Microcontroller: STM32 H7, Cortex-M7
- Clock speed: Up to 480 MHz
- Memory: 2 MB flash, 1 MB RAM
- Redundancy: 3 IMUs, 2 barometers
- Size: 92 x 54 x 14.6 mm
- Weight: 63 g

**Best for**

- Agriculture
- Survey and mapping
- Defense and security
- Education and research

**FAQ**

- **What redundancy does the Aazmuth flight controller have?** Aazmuth carries 3 IMUs and 2 barometers, each on its own isolated bus, so a fault or vibration on one sensor does not corrupt the others.
- **What processor does Aazmuth use?** An STM32 H7 (Cortex-M7) running at up to 480 MHz, with 2 MB of flash and 1 MB of RAM.
- **How big and heavy is it?** 92 x 54 x 14.6 mm and 63 g.
- **Where are the pinout and calibration guides?** In our public documentation. Overview, pinout, calibration, sensor integration and dimensions are all linked on this page.

- **Can I buy in bulk or for a company or OEM project?** Yes. Send quantity and use case through the quote form, or call or WhatsApp us, and we will reply with the right parts.

- **Do you ship across India?** Call or WhatsApp us with your pincode and quantity and we will confirm shipping.
