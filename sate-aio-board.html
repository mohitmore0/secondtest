SATE-AIO Board

Flight controllers

SATE-AIO is an all-in-one board that combines an STM32 flight controller, a 4th-generation IMU, an industrial-grade barometer and an integrated 4-in-1 ESC rated up to 25 A continuous. It is AM32 compatible with DShot support, for compact FPV and multirotor builds.

Highlights

  • STM32 microcontroller for fast, low-latency flight control
  • Single 4th-generation IMU for stability and noise rejection
  • Industrial-grade barometer for altitude sensing
  • Integrated 4-in-1 ESC and power system
  • AM32 compatible with DShot protocol support

SATE-AIO Board FAQ

What is an AIO board?

One board that holds both the flight controller and the 4-in-1 ESC, so there is less wiring and a smaller stack.

What current can it handle?

The integrated ESC is rated up to 25 A continuous.
