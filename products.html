Drone flight controllers, ESCs and GPS modules

Space and Tech Engineering LLP is an Indian drone electronics company with offices in Mumbai and Nashik. It designs and sells the Aazmuth and Exelr8 flight controllers, Skydrive AM32 ESCs and NAVSATE GPS modules for UAV and FPV builders.

Flight controllers

Aazmuth flight controller

Modular, triple-redundant STM32 H7 flight controller with 3 IMUs and 2 barometers on isolated buses.

Exelr8 FPV flight controller F7

F7 flight controller for light, fast FPV racing and freestyle builds.

ESCs

Skydrive AM32 60A 3-6S

60A AM32 ESC for 3-6S LiPo builds. Handles heavy payloads such as sprayer drones.

Skydrive AM32 40A 3-6S

40A AM32 ESC for 3-6S LiPo builds. A light choice for survey and mapping drones.

Skydrive 55A AM32 4-in-1

Four 55A AM32 ESCs on one board. Short wiring for 5-inch FPV builds and below.
