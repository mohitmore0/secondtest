SATE 200A AM32 ESC for 6-12S Drones

ESCs

SATE-HIGH AMP ESC is a high-current single ESC for UAV propulsion, with a stated 200 A continuous drain and a 6S-12S input range. It runs AM32 firmware on an STM microcontroller and includes crash protection plus reverse-voltage and short-circuit protection.

Highlights

  • 200A continuous drain (as stated in catalogue)
  • Crash-protection feature
  • Reverse-voltage and short-circuit protection
  • AM32 firmware supported
  • Wide compatibility and customization

What to check before ordering

Request the rating conditions and confirm cooling and connector requirements using your motor, propeller and battery details. Use your measured current under load when comparing ESCs.
