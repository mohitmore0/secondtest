What is AM32 firmware?

AM32 is open-source firmware for 32-bit brushless ESCs. Skydrive ESCs run it, so you can configure and update them with the usual AM32 tools.

Which ESC should I buy for an agriculture or sprayer drone?

Start with the Skydrive AM32 60A 3-6S, which is meant for heavier payloads. Share your motor, prop and battery setup and we will confirm the fit.

Which flight controller should I pick?

Aazmuth for redundancy and professional work, Exelr8 F7 for FPV builds. Tell us your build and we will confirm.
