Aazmuth Mini STM32H7 Flight Controller

Flight controllers - coming soon

A low-profile STM32H7 flight controller for training drones. It combines an IMU, barometer and magnetometer in a compact enclosure.

Aazmuth Mini is a compact, budget-friendly flight controller for training drones. It uses an STM32H7 with fourth-generation InvenSense inertial sensing, a MEAS barometer and an industry-grade magnetometer, inside a low-profile case designed to minimise airflow disturbance in flight.

Highlights

  • Cost-effective design with low-profile form factor
  • Budget-friendly solution
  • External casing designed to minimize airflow disturbance

What to confirm before launch

For a training fleet, check mounting options, cable access and firmware support for the exercises you plan to teach. Include the number of aircraft when requesting a quote.

Aazmuth Mini FAQ

What is Aazmuth Mini for?

Training drones and compact UAV platforms where a low-profile case helps.

Aazmuth or Aazmuth Mini?

Aazmuth is the triple-redundant flagship. Aazmuth Mini is the compact, lower-cost option for training.
