Skydrive 55A AM32 4-in-1 FAQ

What is a 4-in-1 ESC?

A single board that carries four speed controllers, one for each motor of a quadcopter, which keeps wiring short and weight low.

Which firmware does the 55A 4-in-1 run?

AM32.
