1. How to file a complaint

Write to the Grievance Officer by email, phone or post using the details above. Tell us the page or link, what the problem is and how to reach you. Complaints about content help us most when they include the exact web address.

2. How fast we act

  • Acknowledgement of every complaint: within 24 hours.
  • Resolution of a complaint: within seven days of receipt.
  • Requests to remove content that breaches our Terms of Use (other than ownership, infringement and general-law categories): as quickly as possible and within 36 hours.
  • Intimate or impersonating content: if a person, or someone on their behalf, complains that content shows their private area, full or partial nudity, a sexual act, or is an impersonation including a morphed image, we take reasonable and practicable steps to remove or disable access within two hours of the complaint.
  • Orders of a court or lawful notices from the Appropriate Government: acted on within three hours of receipt, as the rules require.

3. If you are not satisfied

If you disagree with the Grievance Officer's decision, or you do not get a resolution in time, you may appeal to the Grievance Appellate Committee established by the Central Government within 30 days of receiving our reply. The Committee aims to decide within 30 calendar days, and its orders bind us.

4. Law enforcement and government agencies

Lawful orders and notices can be sent to the Grievance Officer at the address above. We provide information or assistance to an authorised agency within 72 hours of a written order that states its purpose.

5. Our commitment

We respect the rights of every person under the Constitution of India, including Articles 14, 19 and 21, and we keep our services accessible and transparent.
