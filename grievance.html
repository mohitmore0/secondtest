1. How to complain

Write, call or email the Grievance Officer using the details above. Please include your name, contact details, the page or product concerned and what went wrong. Complaints about privacy or personal data go to the same officer.

2. What you can expect

  • We acknowledge your complaint within 2 working days.
  • We aim to resolve it within 15 days of receipt, or tell you why more time is needed.
  • We give you the reason for our decision in writing.

3. Scope of this policy

The timelines above are our own service commitments. This website does not host or publish content uploaded by the public. If any law gives you a shorter timeline or a further appeal route in a particular situation, we will follow it.

4. Consumer complaints

If you are a consumer and are not satisfied, you may also contact the National Consumer Helpline (1915) or the consumer commission that has jurisdiction under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.

5. Government and law-enforcement requests

Lawful notices and orders may be sent in writing to the Grievance Officer at the postal address above.
