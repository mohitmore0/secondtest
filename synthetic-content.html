AI and Synthetic Content Policy

Under the IT Rules, audio, images and video that are artificially created or altered so they look real are called synthetically generated information. This is our policy on it.

Last updated: 16 November 2026

1. What we label

Any image, video or audio on this site that was created or materially altered with AI or other computer generation, and could be taken for a real person, place or event, carries a clear visible label (or an audio notice at the start) and, where technically possible, embedded provenance information. Product renders, diagrams and illustrations are marked as illustrative where they are not photographs of the actual product.

2. What we do not allow

  • synthetic content showing child sexual abuse material, non-consensual intimate imagery, or anything obscene, pornographic or sexually explicit;
  • false documents or false electronic records;
  • content about making explosives, arms or ammunition;
  • fake depictions of a real person or event meant to deceive, including false identity, voice, actions or statements.

3. Routine edits are fine

Normal editing such as cropping, colour correction, noise reduction, compression, translation, captions and accessibility improvements, and good-faith drafts, templates and educational material, do not need a label so long as they do not change the real meaning.

4. Report a concern

If you see content on our site that looks fake, misleading or harmful, send the link to the Grievance Officer. We act quickly, remove or disable access where needed and keep the evidence intact.
