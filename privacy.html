Privacy Policy

We keep this simple: we collect only what we need to answer your enquiry, quote you, deliver an order and support you after the sale.

Last updated: 16 November 2026

1. What we collect

Only what you give us: name, phone number, email, product of interest, build details and message when you use our contact form, email or WhatsApp, plus details needed to raise an invoice or ship an order (company name, address, GST number). The contact form opens your own email app or WhatsApp with the message prepared; nothing is sent until you press send there.

2. Why we use it

  • to reply to enquiries and prepare quotes;
  • to process orders, invoices and warranty or support requests;
  • to meet legal, tax and security obligations;
  • to share product updates only if you ask for them.

3. Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with logistics, payment and accounting partners who need it to serve you, and with government agencies when a lawful order requires it. Where a lawful written order requires, we provide information to an authorised agency within the time the rules allow.

4. How long we keep it

Enquiry and registration details are kept for as long as needed for the purpose above. If we collect registration information from you, we retain it for 180 days after you cancel or withdraw it. Content removed under our terms and its related records are preserved for 180 days, or longer if a court or authorised agency requires.

5. Security

We follow reasonable security practices and procedures under the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Information) Rules, 2011, and report cyber security incidents to CERT-In as required.

6. Your choices

You may ask us to correct or delete your details, or to stop contacting you, by writing to the Grievance Officer. We will acknowledge within 24 hours and resolve within seven days.

7. Third-party links

Our site links to services such as our documentation, LinkedIn and Instagram. Their privacy practices are their own.
