Privacy Policy

We collect only what we need to answer your enquiry and supply our products. This policy explains what, why, and how you stay in control.

Effective: 16 November 2026. Last updated: 16 November 2026.

1. Who is responsible

Space and Tech Engineering LLP (registered office: Unit 37A, Patel Industrial Estate, Vivekananda Rd, Yadav Nagar, Dahisar East, Mumbai 400068) decides why and how your personal data is used. Privacy contact: the Grievance Officer listed on the Grievance Redressal page.

2. Which law applies today

The Information Technology Act, 2000 and the SPDI Rules, 2011 apply now. The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025 are being brought into force in phases; as published, the main duties on businesses (notice, consent, rights and breach reporting) apply from May 2027. We are shaping this policy and our forms to match them, but we do not claim compliance with provisions that are not yet in force.

3. What we collect

  • Contact form or message: name, phone number, product of interest and the message you write (build details, quantity).
  • Email or WhatsApp: the details your account shows us and what you send.
  • Orders and invoices: company name, billing and shipping address, GSTIN and similar details needed to supply and bill.
  • We do not ask for sensitive data such as health or financial account details through this site.

4. Why we use it, and on what basis

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a quotation: you ask us to, and you consent by sending the message.
  • To supply, invoice, deliver and support products: to perform the contract with you.
  • To meet legal, tax, accounting and security obligations.
  • We do not send marketing messages unless you separately ask us to. We never pre-tick consent boxes.

5. How the contact form works

The form does not store your details on our servers. When you submit it, it opens your own email app or WhatsApp with the message ready, and nothing is sent until you press send there. Details are then handled in those services under their own policies.

6. Cookies, analytics and third-party resources

As of the effective date this site sets no advertising or analytics cookies. To display the page, your browser loads fonts from Google and some product images from servers hosted by Wix, so those providers receive your IP address and browser details. If we add analytics or advertising cookies later, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first where required.

7. Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us (email, messaging, courier, accounting, hosting) and with authorities when the law or a lawful order requires it.

8. Retention and security

We keep enquiry and order records only as long as needed for the purpose, and for as long as tax, accounting or other law requires. We use reasonable security safeguards suited to the data we handle. If a personal data breach affects you, we will inform you and the authorities as the law requires.

9. Your rights and withdrawing consent

You can ask us to give you a summary of your data, correct or complete it, erase it, or stop using it, and you can withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawal is as easy as giving it: write to the Grievance Officer and we will act, except where we must keep data by law. Withdrawing does not affect what was done before. You may also nominate someone to exercise these rights for you if you cannot.

10. Children

This site is meant for business and adult users. Please do not send us personal data of anyone under 18.

11. Complaints

Write to the Grievance Officer first. If you are not satisfied, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India once its complaint provisions are in force, or any other authority the law provides.
