Kinetik FPV Flight Controller

Flight controllers

A 5 x 5 cm STM32H7 flight controller for 3S to 6S builds, with two IMUs, two barometers and support for Betaflight and ArduPilot.

Kinetik FC is a compact, FPV-based flight controller designed for drone enthusiasts, developers, and UAV builders. Measuring just 5 × 5 cm, it supports both Betaflight and ArduPilot, offering the flexibility to power everything from FPV freestyle and racing drones to autonomous UAVs. Its standout feature is dual firmware compatibility, giving users the freedom to choose between high-performance FPV flying and advanced autonomous flight on a single platform.
