Exelr8 F7 FPV Flight Controller

Flight controllers

An F7 flight controller for 3S to 6S racing and freestyle quads, with an onboard OSD and separate 5 V and 10 V outputs for supported peripherals.

Exelr8 is an F7 flight controller for 3S to 6S FPV racing and freestyle builds. It combines a fourth-generation InvenSense IMU, an Infineon barometer and onboard OSD. Separate 5 V and 10 V BEC outputs can power compatible peripherals within their listed ratings.

Highlights

  • F7 processor
  • Pairs with Skydrive 4-in-1 ESC and NAVSATE mini GPS
  • Designed and supported in India

What to check before ordering

Check frame mounting, the ESC cable pinout and the voltage and current requirements of your receiver and video system. Ask which current ESC options fit your stack if you are replacing an older board.

Exelr8 FPV flight controller F7 FAQ

What is the Exelr8 flight controller for?

Exelr8 is an F7 flight controller for FPV drone builds, including racing and freestyle quads.

Which ESC and GPS go with Exelr8?

Choose an ESC that matches your battery and motor load, then check the harness pinout. If you are adding a GPS such as the NAVSATE Mini, confirm the interface and firmware support for your build.

Exelr8 or Aazmuth?

Choose Exelr8 for FPV builds and Aazmuth when you need triple-redundant sensing for professional or mission work.
