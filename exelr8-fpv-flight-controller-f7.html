Exelr8 FPV flight controller F7 FAQ

What is the Exelr8 flight controller for?

Exelr8 is an F7 flight controller for FPV drone builds, including racing and freestyle quads.

Which ESC and GPS go with Exelr8?

The Skydrive 55A AM32 4-in-1 ESC and the NAVSATE mini GPS are the matching parts in our FPV stack.

Exelr8 or Aazmuth?

Choose Exelr8 for FPV builds and Aazmuth when you need triple-redundant sensing for professional or mission work.
