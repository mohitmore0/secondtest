How to choose a GPS module for UAVs and FPV drones

By Space and Tech Engineering LLP | Updated 2026-11-25

Quick answer: For reliable return-to-home and position hold, choose a GNSS module with multi-constellation support, a good antenna and a compass if your firmware needs one.

In this guide
  1. GPS or GNSS?
  2. What to look for
  3. Mounting tips
  4. Full size or mini?
  5. FAQ

GPS or GNSS?

GPS is the American satellite system. GNSS is the general term that includes GPS, GLONASS, Galileo and BeiDou. A drone module that listens to several constellations can lock more satellites and gets a stable position faster.

What to look for

  • Multi-constellation support for faster lock and better accuracy.
  • Update rate: higher rates give smoother position data to the flight controller.
  • Compass (magnetometer): needed on many multirotors for heading, and it must be kept away from power wires.
  • Antenna size and placement: a bigger patch antenna on top of the frame usually gives better reception.
  • Connector and protocol: make sure it matches the flight controller UART and firmware, such as INAV or ArduPilot.

Mounting tips

Mount the module on a mast or the top plate with a clear view of the sky. Keep it away from the VTX antenna and high-current wires. Wait for a good satellite lock before arming, and test return-to-home at a safe height first.

Full size or mini?

Full-size modules suit mapping, agriculture and long-range builds where reliability matters. Mini modules save weight for compact FPV drones and cinewhoops.

Frequently asked questions

How many satellites do I need for a safe flight?

More is better. Many pilots wait for a solid lock with a healthy count before arming, and always test return-to-home before relying on it.

Does an FPV drone need GPS?

Not for freestyle. For long range flights, GPS enables return-to-home and position logging.

Why is my GPS slow to lock?

Poor sky view, interference from the VTX or power wires, or no cold-start almanac can slow the first lock. Try an open area and wait a few minutes.
