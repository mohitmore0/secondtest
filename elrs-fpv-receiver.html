2.4 GHz ExpressLRS FPV Receiver (ELRS)

Receivers

A 2.4 GHz ExpressLRS receiver for FPV aircraft. It connects to the flight controller through CRSF, takes a 5 V supply and includes a 65 mm T-antenna.

The ELRS receiver is an ExpressLRS-approved 2.4 GHz receiver for FPV aircraft. It uses an ESP32 microcontroller with a Semtech RF chip and a built-in TCXO, and connects to the flight controller over the CRSF bus. It gives a fast, smooth link with telemetry back to the radio.

Highlights

  • ESP32 microcontroller
  • ExpressLRS-approved design
  • Fast and smooth connection
  • Built-in TCXO
  • CRSF bus interface

What to check before ordering

Check that your radio uses 2.4 GHz ExpressLRS, that the flight controller has a suitable UART for CRSF and that the frame has room for the antenna. Confirm firmware compatibility before binding.
