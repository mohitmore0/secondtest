ELRS FPV radio receiver

Receivers

The ELRS receiver is an ExpressLRS-approved 2.4 GHz receiver for FPV aircraft. It uses an ESP32 microcontroller with a Semtech RF chip and a built-in TCXO, and connects to the flight controller over the CRSF bus. It gives a fast, smooth link with telemetry back to the radio.

Highlights

  • ESP32 microcontroller
  • ExpressLRS-approved design
  • Fast and smooth connection
  • Built-in TCXO
  • CRSF bus interface

ELRS FPV radio receiver specifications
